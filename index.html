Quick rules reference

Long-Limbed: melee reach is 10 ft. on Vargo's own turn.

Reckless: choose on the first STR-based melee weapon attack of the turn; STR melee weapon attacks for the rest of the turn have advantage. Attacks against Vargo have advantage until his next turn.

Ancestral Protectors: while raging, the first creature Vargo hits on his turn is affected until the start of Vargo's next turn. It has disadvantage attacking anyone except Vargo; if it hits someone else, that target has resistance to all damage dealt by that attack.

GWM bonus attack: one melee weapon attack as a Bonus Action after a melee crit or reducing a creature to 0 HP with a melee weapon, only if the Bonus Action is still free.
